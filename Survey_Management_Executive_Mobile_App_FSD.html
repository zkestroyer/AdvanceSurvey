Functional Specification Document (FSD)
Survey Management / Executive Mobile Application

1. Overview

The Survey Management Mobile Application shall be developed for Executive Management, Regional Managers, Sales Heads and other authorized management users to monitor survey activities, compare market intelligence, analyze product performance and make business decisions directly from their mobile devices.

Unlike the Survey App used by field staff for data collection, the Management App shall be a read-only analytical application providing dashboards, reports, comparisons and business insights.

15. Permissions

Management users shall have read-only access.

  • View all surveys
  • View all reports
  • View dashboards
  • View comparisons
  • View graphs
  • View price history
  • View shop details
  • Drill down into survey records

Management users shall not be allowed to:

  • Create surveys
  • Edit surveys
  • Delete surveys
  • Add shops
  • Modify product data
  • Submit survey responses
