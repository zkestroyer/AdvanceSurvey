Comparison Reports

A dedicated Comparison Reports module shall be developed within the Web Portal.

1. Comparison Report Filters

The Comparison Reports module shall support the following filters:

  • Product Category
  • Brand
  • Product
  • Distributor
  • Warranty
  • IP Rating
  • Capacity
  • Energy
  • Territory
  • City
  • Area
  • Date Range
  • Survey Type
  • Any future configurable survey field

The Product Price Comparison shall support:

  • Compare Purchase Prices side-by-side.
  • View Shop Count.
  • Sort Highest to Lowest / Lowest to Highest.
  • Drill down to survey records.

3. Price History

The system shall maintain and display the following pricing information:

  • Historical Purchase Prices
  • Historical Selling Prices
  • Current Purchase Price
  • Current Selling Price
  • Price Difference
  • Price Change Timeline
  • Effective Date

4. Comparison Engine

Management users shall be able to perform the same dynamic comparisons described in Section 5 from both the Web Portal and the Mobile Application.

The Comparison Engine shall support:

  • Compare Products
  • Compare Brands
  • Compare Distributors
  • Compare Purchase Prices
  • Compare Selling Prices
  • Compare Territories
  • Compare Cities
  • Compare Product Categories
  • Compare any configurable survey field

5. Comparison Results

The comparison results shall support the following capabilities:

  • Highest/Lowest sorting
  • Graphical View
  • Tabular View
  • Drill-down into survey records
  • Historical Pricing Timeline
  • Historical Price Comparison
  • Shop Count
